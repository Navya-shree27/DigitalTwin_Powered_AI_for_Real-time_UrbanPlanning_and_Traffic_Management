# Engineering Analysis Reports

This folder holds automatically generated microscopic traffic engineering analysis reports for scenarios and validated proposals.

## Available Reports
- `current_network_report.md`: Baseline capacity metrics, flow rates, and Level of Service (LOS) grade.
- `congestion_report.md`: Identified road bottlenecks, congestion ratios, and queues.
- `signal_analysis.md`: Detailed approach stopped delay and signal index evaluations.
- `recommended_changes.md`: Specific timing or lane modifications proposed by AI agents.
- `best_scenario.md`: Side-by-side comparative validation (before-vs-after deltas) of the changes.
